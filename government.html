How an engagement runs.

You work with the founder directly. Specialist nurse leaders join only for the phases that need them, and only after they are named in writing in the proposal.

  1. Scope in writing

    Deliverables, schedule and named specialists, agreed before work begins.

  2. Gap analysis

    Where the program stands against the standard, stated plainly.

  3. Tracer rehearsal

    Teams practice the questions surveyors ask on the units.

  4. Evidence and documentation

    Policies, records and narratives built with your staff.

  5. Site-visit practice

    Mock review and walk-throughs before the real visit.
