CPHQ is a credential of the National Association for Healthcare Quality (NAHQ). CPPS is a credential of the Certification Board for Professionals in Patient Safety (CBPPS). CPHRM is a credential of the AHA Certification Center. Each body sets its own eligibility rules and runs its own exam. Nursing Edge Advisory offers independent exam preparation only. We do not issue these credentials, we do not guarantee exam results, and we are not sponsored by, endorsed by, or affiliated with NAHQ, CBPPS, or the AHA Certification Center.
